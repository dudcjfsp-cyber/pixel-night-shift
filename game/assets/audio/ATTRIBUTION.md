CC0 does not require attribution. These details are retained to make provenance and future replacement audits explicit.

## CC0 sound effects

All runtime files under `sfx/` are selected and renamed final files from Kenney’s **Digital Audio** pack.

- Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/digital-audio
- Author: Kenney
- License: CC0 1.0 Universal

The exact runtime-to-source filename mapping is recorded once in `manifest.json`. The downloaded pack archive and unused candidates are intentionally excluded from Git.

## Project-original audio

`bgm/maintenance_loop.wav` is deterministic procedural synthesis generated by `res://tools/generate_audio.gd`. It contains no recordings, samples, or external melodies.
